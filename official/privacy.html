Privacy

Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 17, 2026 · LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 17, 2026 · PRODUCT: SUPERK (CREATED BY KABALI GAMER)

1. Our Commitment

SuperK was built by a student, for students. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data, usage metrics, or browsing activity. The extension has no servers of its own, so there is nothing for the developer to collect. The only personal data we ever receive is what you choose to send through the Feedback form (Section 7).

2. Scope

This policy covers (a) the SuperK browser extension and (b) this website. Section 7 explains the website separately.

3. What the Extension Accesses and Why

  • a) Active Tab (activeTab): used only when you click the extension, to check that you are on an authorized Parul University classroom page (elearning.paruluniversity.ac.in).
  • b) Scripting (scripting): used only to run the one-time scan that lists the files on the classroom page you are viewing.
  • c) Storage (storage): used only on your device (chrome.storage.local) to keep the list of discovered PDF titles and download progress during your session. This data never leaves your device.
  • d) Downloads (downloads): used only to save the PDF or ZIP files you request into your local Downloads/Parul/ folder.
  • e) Host permissions: elearning.paruluniversity.ac.in (to work with the classroom page); platform-api.edorer.com (to read your subject syllabus using your existing session); *.digitaloceanspaces.com (to download the authorized files from the university's cloud storage).

4. Authentication and Credentials

SuperK never sees or stores your password. When you log in to the university portal, your browser keeps a session token in its localStorage. SuperK uses that existing token in memory only to request your own subject list and files from the university's API. It does not copy, save, or send the token anywhere else.

5. Third-Party Servers

When you use SuperK, your browser connects directly to the university's learning portal, its API provider (Edorer), and its cloud file storage (DigitalOcean Spaces). Those parties may see standard technical information such as your IP address and request details, as they do whenever you use the portal normally. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies, not this one. The developer receives none of it.

6. No Analytics or Tracking

SuperK contains: no Google Analytics or similar trackers, no user fingerprinting, no advertising networks, no remote logging or crash-report servers, and no accounts.

7. This Website

This website does not use analytics or advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may send your IP address and standard request data to Google when you visit. Our host, Google Firebase Hosting, may keep standard server logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and operation. Pages that show the demo video embed a YouTube player, which may set its own cookies once loaded. See our Cookie Statement for details. Links to GitHub and Instagram lead to third-party sites with their own policies. Tips are voluntary payments made through your own UPI app; this site does not receive your payment details.

Feedback form. When you send a report through our Feedback page, the form sends the following to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a database service used by the developer: your name, email address, optional WhatsApp/phone number, the message you write, an optional screenshot link, an optional star rating, any symptom boxes you tick, and the address of the page you submitted from. Your browser and operating system, and the extension version (when you arrive from the extension), are attached automatically to help reproduce the issue. This is used solely to read and act on your report, and is visible only to the developer — never published. Each report is automatically deleted 6 months after it is sent (deletion happens within about a day of that date), and we may delete it sooner once the issue is handled. You can ask for deletion at any time via the contact details below.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All data SuperK stores lives on your device. You can remove it by clearing the extension's storage or uninstalling the extension. Downloaded files are saved in your Downloads/Parul/ folder, and you can delete them at any time.

9. Children

SuperK is intended for university students and instructors and is not directed at children under 18. The developer knowingly collects no data from children.

10. Your Rights

The extension collects nothing from you, so there is no extension data to access, correct, or erase. Reports you send through the Feedback page (Section 7) can be deleted on request at any time. If you still have a privacy question or request (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable), contact us and we will respond in good faith.

11. Open Source and Transparency

SuperK's source code is public at https://github.com/kabaliGamer-Dev2/superk so you can verify what it does.

12. Security

Everything runs locally in your browser and uses the secure (HTTPS) connections of the university's own services. No method is 100% secure, so please keep your login details private.

13. Changes to This Policy

If SuperK's permissions or behavior change, this policy will be updated and the effective date changed. Check it when you update the extension.

14. Contact

Questions or security disclosures: kabaligamer.dev@proton.me or https://github.com/kabaliGamer-Dev2/superk/issues
