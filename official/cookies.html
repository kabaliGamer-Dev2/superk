Cookie & Storage Policy

1. Website Cookies

The official SuperK landing website uses zero tracking cookies, no marketing cookies, and no third-party analytic trackers. Some third-party resources can still connect your browser to their servers when a page loads or a feature is used:

  • Google Fonts — every page loads its typefaces from Google, so your IP address and standard request data reach Google.
  • YouTube — the homepage embeds the demo video through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which is designed to avoid setting cookies until you press play. The player itself may still set its own cookies once a video is played.
  • Firebase (Cloud Firestore) — the Feedback page sends what you write there (name, email, optional phone, message, and the attached technical details — see our Privacy Policy) to Google Firebase so the developer can read it. This uses no cookies.
  • Local storage flags (this website) — the landing site keeps a few small keys in your browser's localStorage: superk.coffee.shown (so the one-time "support SuperK" tip card is never shown twice), superk.fb.last (blocks accidental duplicate feedback submissions for a short window), and superk.gate.terms.v1 / superk.gate.instagram.v1 (remember that you completed the download-gate steps). They contain no identifiers, never leave your browser, and disappear when you clear site data.

2. Browser Storage in Extension

  • chrome.storage.local: SuperK saves temporary session keys (pu_items, pu_current, pu_classroomId) exclusively in your browser's isolated local storage so the popup renders instantly without re-fetching data every second.
  • localStorage interaction: SuperK reads the university's existing authentication session (edorer.auth.session) in read-only mode to authenticate API requests on your behalf. SuperK never writes to or modifies your portal storage.

3. How to Clear Stored Data

You can clear cached data at any time by right-clicking SuperK in your extensions toolbar and choosing "Remove from Chrome", or clicking Rescan inside the popup.
