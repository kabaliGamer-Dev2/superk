Quick install via unpacked extension

Five steps, about thirty seconds.

Download

Click Download SuperK.zip and unzip it somewhere safe (e.g. Documents/SuperK).

Open extensions page

Chrome: chrome://extensions
Brave: brave://extensions
Edge: edge://extensions

Enable Developer mode

Toggle the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner of the page.

Load unpacked

Click Load unpacked (top-left) and select the unzipped extension folder.

Pin SuperK

Click the puzzle icon in your toolbar, then pin SuperK so it's one click away.

How to use on the Parul LMS

Your classroom session is detected automatically — nothing to configure.

01

Open your classroom

Go to elearning.paruluniversity.ac.in, log in, and open any course: /classrooms/<id>/student.

02

Click the SuperK icon

Open any subject in the page, then click SuperK in your toolbar. It locks onto that subject automatically.

03

Download all

Click Download all for the whole subject as one ZIP, or tick files and click Selected.

Common troubleshooting

The two answers every student needs.

The popup says "Page bridge is outdated — refresh tab"?

This happens if you reloaded the extension while the LMS tab was already open. Simply press F5 / Refresh on your LMS tab and reopen SuperK.

What if a subject has over 150MB of PDFs?

SuperK has an automatic memory safeguard. Past ~150MB it skips the single ZIP and downloads the files individually instead — same PDFs, zero browser crashes.
