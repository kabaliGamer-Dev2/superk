Good questions

Before you tap pay

Is contributing required to use SuperK?

No. Contributions are entirely optional and every SuperK feature stays available under the project's normal terms. Nothing is gated behind payment.

Where does my contribution go?

It may go toward development, bug fixes, browser compatibility, or legitimate project costs. These are potential uses, not a fixed allocation, and no donor receives anything in return.

Is my payment secure?

Payment happens entirely inside your own UPI app with your bank — SuperK's website never handles your UPI PIN, payment password or card details. This site stores no payment records and cannot confirm that a payment completed.

Can I contribute more than once?

Yes. Each payment is a separate one-time transaction handled by your UPI app. Contribute as often as you'd like.

What if my payment fails or I change my mind?

If the payment fails, retry through your UPI app. If you paid by mistake, contact kabaligamer.dev@proton.me and we will help. Terms §17 applies to all contributions.

Can I suggest a feature instead?

Absolutely — that costs nothing. The feedback page is where ideas and bug reports go, and they are read either way.
