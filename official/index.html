What SuperK does

Six reasons students install it

Everything runs inside your browser, on your machine, using the classroom session you already have open.

01

One-Click Subject ZIP Packaging

Why download 30 files one-by-one? SuperK groups all PPTs, Lecture Notes, Lab Manuals, and Question Papers into topic folders (8. PPTs/, 6. Assignments/) and generates an organized .zip file instantly.

02

Smart Automatic Filename Resolution

Faculty frequently title every document generically as "PPT" or "Assignment". SuperK automatically inspects the cloud storage slug, detects the real unit name (e.g. Unit 1 - Graph Theory), and numbers duplicates neatly.

03

Paced & Safe Downloads

Never trigger browser crashes or cloud rate-limits. SuperK uses an intelligent 350ms throttle queue that guarantees smooth, reliable disk writes.

04

Zero Account Setup

No secondary logins or new accounts. SuperK runs securely in your browser and automatically detects your active classroom session.

05

100% Private & Client-Side

Zero telemetry. Zero analytics. No remote servers. Your authentication tokens and PDF requests never leave your own machine.

06

Built-in Quick Filter (⌘K / Ctrl+K)

Instant debounced search. Type any unit, topic, or document title to filter through hundreds of classroom documents in milliseconds.

Quick answers

Asked by every student

Straight answers, no fine print.

Why did my ZIP download say "Too Big"?

Your browser tab only has so much RAM. SuperK measures every file first, and past ~150 MB it skips the single ZIP and downloads the files individually instead — same PDFs, zero crashes.

Why does it say "No Auth" or fallback mode?

Your classroom login session hadn't loaded when SuperK scanned. Refresh the Edorer classroom tab once so the session token loads, then reopen SuperK.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera, and Arc — any Chromium browser that supports Manifest V3 extensions.
