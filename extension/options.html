Why SuperK exists

"When our LMS update removed every PDF download link, I built SuperK so no student has to struggle before exams. One click, every PDF, beautifully." — Kabali Gamer

SuperK lists and bulk-downloads PDFs from your Parul University elearning classroom, grouping them into topic folders and packing whole subjects into one ZIP.

How to use

  1. Open elearning.paruluniversity.ac.in/classrooms/…/student and log in.
  2. Open any subject, then click the SuperK toolbar icon — it locks onto that subject.
  3. Click Download all for the whole subject as a ZIP, or tick files and click Selected.

Tip: type in the search box (or press Ctrl+K / ⌘K) to filter hundreds of files instantly.

Troubleshooting

  • "Page bridge is outdated — refresh tab": the extension was reloaded while the classroom tab was already open. Press F5 on the classroom tab and reopen SuperK.
  • Huge subjects (~150MB+): SuperK automatically switches from one ZIP to rapid individual downloads to protect browser memory.
  • "No PDFs yet": open a subject topic page (PPT / Notes / Previous papers), then press Rescan.

Privacy guarantee

Zero-Data Collection. No trackers, no analytics, no remote servers. Your login session token never leaves your own browser — requests go only to the official university LMS infrastructure (platform-api.edorer.com, *.digitaloceanspaces.com).
